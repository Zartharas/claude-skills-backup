# Source Quality and Freshness

## Preferred evidence order

1. official current product docs;
2. official API/schema/source repository;
3. official KB/support article;
4. official vendor engineering/threat research;
5. maintainer-authored forum response;
6. community accepted answer;
7. independent engineering blog;
8. unverified forum/social claim.

## Community sources

Use forums and blogs for:
- undocumented failure patterns;
- practical workarounds;
- field experience;
- hypothesis generation.

Do not use them as sole authority for:
- supportability;
- licensing;
- security advisories;
- current syntax;
- compatibility;
- end-of-life status.

## Version-sensitive claims

Always bind the claim to:
- product;
- edition/cloud/on-prem context;
- version/release when relevant;
- source date;
- current documentation.

## Threat research

Threat-research blogs can establish observed attacker behavior and detection ideas.
They do not establish product configuration correctness.
